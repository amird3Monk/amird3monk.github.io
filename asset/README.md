Put your images and your 3D model in this folder.

Names the page already expects (change them in index.html if you prefer others):

  ga-drawing.png         general arrangement drawing
  pulley-drawing.png     drive pulley fabrication drawing
  render-assembly.png    rendered assembly, three-quarter view
  render-drive-end.png   close-up of the coupling and bearing
  conveyor.glb           3D model — the viewer loads this on its own

Sizes:
  images   1600 px wide is plenty. Save as PNG for drawings, JPG for renders.
  model    keep the .glb under about 5 MB or phones will struggle.
           Export it WITHOUT Draco compression.
